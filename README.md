A single sentence that explains a modern thing — a video call — to a
traveler from 1800, using only words and concepts that existed before
1800. A small mirror of dark glass holds the living face of someone
three thousand miles away, and her portrait answers when spoken to.

Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800" bounty, $2 in USDC.

## Entry

> A small mirror of dark glass holds the living face of your wife, three thousand miles away, and when you speak, her portrait answers you at once.

Run `main.py` to write the entry to `entry.txt` (verified byte-for-byte).
